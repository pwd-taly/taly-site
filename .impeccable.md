## Design Context

### Users
People visiting Taly's public developer home, usually to understand who they are and find their GitHub work.

### Brand Personality
Direct, curious, technical. The site should feel like a thoughtful personal workspace rather than a corporate profile.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial light interface with warm paper-like neutrals, a single burnt-orange signal color, and expressive serif display type. It avoids generic SaaS cards, dark neon developer aesthetics, and decorative dashboards.

### Design Principles
- Make the person's work and next action obvious within seconds.
- Use restrained, text-led composition over interface chrome.
- Keep the page fast, accessible, and useful on small screens.
- Let modest motion add texture without distracting from content.
